## Modification-date rationale

The same 60 values were present as public BlogPosting `dateModified` metadata in the October 11 parity pass and the bounded October 12 metadata pass. Each value is on or after its locale-specific publication date, and each affected local record is at same-language body parity with the retrieved public page. The date component is therefore preserved as public structured-data metadata without claiming that every change was a substantive editorial rewrite. HTTP `Last-Modified` and HubSpot prerender timestamps remain separate evidence fields and were not copied into frontmatter.

The Arabic `/ar/library/aba-school-readiness-guide` record is the sole unresolved case. Phase 6C.2 replaced its body with a new Arabic translation of the current English antecedent/consequence article, while the public Arabic URL still serves the old school-readiness article. Its public `2025-11-22` timestamp cannot be attributed to the new translation and was deliberately not copied.
