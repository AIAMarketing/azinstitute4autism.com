Rula Diab is a passionate clinician and entrepreneur dedicated to supporting children with autism. She founded the Arizona Institute for Autism (AIA) to provide high-quality, individualized ABA therapy in a nurturing and culturally competent environment. Committed to integrity, empowerment, and excellence, Rula ensures that AIA prioritizes both learner success and staff development. Her vision stems from a deep desire to help children reach their highest potential, creating a positive impact on families and the community. Through perseverance and dedication, she has built AIA into a trusted center for autism care. Beyond her work, Rula enjoys the cultural and natural beauty in and around Scottsdale, from Old Town’s charm to the Desert Botanical Garden and Sedona’s red rock landscapes.
